This **URL to QR Code** tool works 100% in your browser. No URL is sent to ShaSec servers.

Use it to create QR for cafe menus, WiFi, payments, or promos securely. Anti-quishing feature with branded frame and logo helps customers distinguish real vs fake stickers.

**Features:**

- Offline, client-side, no backend
- Size 128-1024px, ECC L/M/Q/H
- Custom foreground/background, quiet zone
- Branded frame with label `shasec.my.id` to prevent sticker replacement
- Center logo (optional) with file upload
- Download PNG, WebP, SVG
- URL validation and phishing detection (IP, @, xn--, HTTP)

> Tip: Print QR on acrylic with logo, check every morning, and use dynamic QR with your own domain if you need scan tracking. See article [Quishing: Evolution of QR Phishing](/en/articles/quishing-evolution-qr-phishing-how-to-spot-malicious-qr/) for full guide.
